TraderTraitor: North Korea's exchange-heist unit

A profile for security and financial-crime teams at exchanges, custodians and wallet providers. It covers who the group is, how it gets in, how it moves the money, and what to watch for.

01Key judgments

  1. By value stolen, TraderTraitor is the most damaging cyber actor in the world today. It is responsible for the four largest thefts attributed to North Korea since 2024: Binance ($6.36B), Bybit ($1.46B), DMM Bitcoin ($308M) and KelpDAO ($292M).12327

    High confidence FBI attributions for Binance, Bybit and DMM; TRM, Chainalysis and LayerZero for KelpDAO.

  2. Its main way in is people, not perimeter exploits. Fake recruiters hand developers and DevOps engineers code to run, and the actor then rides that workstation's cloud credentials toward the systems that sign transactions. In three of the last four major incidents, the victim was reached through a third party: a wallet vendor (Ginco), a wallet software provider (Safe{Wallet}) and bridge infrastructure (LayerZero).12459

    High confidence Consistent across FBI, Safe{Wallet}/Mandiant, Unit 42 and CSA reporting from 2022 to 2026.

  3. The goal is to control what gets signed, not to steal keys. At DMM a real transaction request was altered, at Bybit signers saw a doctored interface, and at Binance the hot wallet's own authority was delegated to the attacker. Key custody controls alone would not have stopped any of these.2410

    Moderate confidence Root causes for DMM and Bybit are public; Binance has not published one, and TRM's view rests on on-chain evidence.

  4. Laundering adapts to whatever can be frozen. After Bybit, ETH moved through bridges and THORChain to Bitcoin. After Binance, freezable stablecoins were sold at a discount of more than 10% within the first hour. Stablecoin issuers and networks froze about $2.7B, which suggests issuer freezes have become the defender's fastest lever.10166

    Moderate confidence TRM's real-time on-chain reporting; the author's own Bybit trace.

  5. Over the next 6 to 12 months, another nine-figure theft is likely. Likely targets are hot-wallet signing systems, multisig governance and cross-chain verifiers, with lures that increasingly use AI tools, AI-themed front companies and developer tooling such as Terraform and AI coding assistants.7913

    Moderate confidence Trend in targets and lures from 2024 to 2026; DPRK's share of hack losses rose from 39% (2024) to 64% (2025) to 76% (early 2026).

Confidence follows standard analytic practice. High means multiple strong, independent sources agree. Moderate means the evidence is credible but has gaps or rests on one type of source. Numbers in boxes link to the graded sources in section 10.

03How an operation unfolds

The pattern has held from 2022 to 2026. The tools change; the route does not. TraderTraitor goes through a person who can run code, then through their credentials, toward whatever produces or approves transactions.

  1. 1TargetPicks developers, DevOps engineers and transaction signers at exchanges and at the vendors that serve them.LinkedIn · Telegram · conferences
  2. 2LureA recruiter, a 'trading firm' or an AI-themed front company sends something to run.Job tests · trading apps · Docker and Terraform projects
  3. 3ExecuteThe target runs it. The payload loads only for validated victims.YAML deserialization · terraform init · Electron update
  4. 4PersistLoaders and macOS implants hide as helpers and talk to C2 over ordinary services.RN Loader · PLOTTWIST · FLATROOF · ROOFDECK
  5. 5Take credentialsKeychains, SSH keys, cloud configs, session cookies and tokens.AWS tokens bypass MFA
  6. 6Reach the signing pathMoves from the workstation to whatever produces or approves transactions.Safe web app · Ginco wallet system · bridge verifier · hot-wallet signer
  7. 7Steal & launderSigners approve a doctored transaction, or the wallet's authority is delegated. Funds leave in minutes.Contract upgrade · EIP-7702 sweep · THORChain · OTC

07Detection and defense playbook

Ordered by where in the operation each control bites. Rules marked sigma/… ship as validated Sigma detections. Behavior-based controls last longer than the indicators in section 08.

Lure & execution

Treat 'take-home tests' as untrusted code

Run any recruiter-supplied repo, Docker image or Terraform project only in a disposable VM with no corporate credentials. Block personal-device access to corporate systems.

Every major TraderTraitor intrusion since 2022 began with an employee running code a stranger sent them.5429

Unexpected provider registries in Terraform runs

Alert on DNS lookups for anything containing 'hashicorp' outside hashicorp.com and terraform.io. Pin providers to the official registry.

The 2026 campaign hid its loader in a typosquatted registry referenced by a lockfile.9

sigma/tt_terraform_registry_typosquat.yml
Shells spawned by AI coding assistants

Log and review child processes of Cursor and similar assistants. Require approval before auto-run tasks in unreviewed workspaces.

FLATROOF and ROOFDECK beaconed only while a Cursor workspace was open.9

sigma/tt_ai_ide_spawns_nohup_shell.yml

Persistence & C2

Fake Chromium helpers and quarantine removal on macOS

Hunt for --type=renderer processes not launched by an installed browser, and for xattr -d com.apple.quarantine on downloaded binaries.

These are ROOFDECK's masquerade and FLATROOF's Gatekeeper bypass.9

sigma/tt_macos_fake_renderer_helper.ymlsigma/tt_macos_quarantine_removal.yml
Telegram and Nostr traffic from engineering laptops

Alert on api.telegram.org and Nostr relay connections from developer and DevOps hosts that have no business need.

Both implants use these services for C2 to blend in.9

sigma/tt_dev_host_telegram_nostr_c2.yml
Known coding-challenge C2 domains

Block and alert on the 27 Unit 42 domains and the 2 registry typosquats. Treat a hit as an incident, not just a block.

These are high-fidelity but short-lived; retire them on a schedule.59

sigma/tt_known_c2_domains.yml

Credential theft & pivot

Short-lived cloud credentials and session binding

Use short session lifetimes, bind sessions to device posture, and alert on developer AWS sessions from VPN exit nodes or unusual user agents.

Hijacked AWS session tokens let the Bybit attackers bypass MFA.4

Signing path

Verify what signers sign, independently

Decode every high-value transaction on a separate device or service before signing, and refuse delegatecall or contract-upgrade operations on treasury wallets without a second channel.

At Bybit and DMM, the theft happened through what signers were shown, not through stolen keys.42

EIP-7702 delegations on treasury wallets

Alert immediately when code appears at a treasury EOA, or when a signing request carries many authorizations across a nonce range. Keep a pre-approved revocation ready.

Binance's Ethereum hot wallet was swept through 50 EIP-7702 authorizations in a single transaction.10

Funds movement

Screen deposits for derived exposure

Screen deposits against the FBI's address lists and their downstream wallets, not only direct matches. Hold BTC arriving from THORChain swaps in the weeks after a major theft.

Unlabeled hop wallets sit one to three hops from listed addresses, and DPRK moves 60% of laundering volume in transfers under $500,000.12166

Pre-arranged freeze contacts

Keep direct escalation paths to Tether, Circle, Ripple and the security councils of major L2s, and practise the call.

Issuers froze about $2.7B of the Binance theft within hours.10

09Intelligence gaps

  • Binance has not published a root cause. Initial access to its signing infrastructure is unknown beyond 'a compromised engineer's laptop' and 'additional points of breach'.
  • The FBI's 628 Binance addresses were not reviewed for this brief; they come from the FBI PDF.
  • No file hashes for FLATROOF, ROOFDECK or RN Stealer were available in the sources reviewed.
  • The relationship between TraderTraitor and the North Korean group behind the Drift theft (TRM: separate subgroup) is unresolved.
  • How much of the Binance theft has been converted to fiat, and through which OTC networks, is not yet known.

11Method and deliverables

Built from open sources only. AI assisted with reading and summarizing reports, mapping behaviors to ATT&CK, and drafting. The STIX bundle was parsed back with the OASIS-aligned stix2 library, and the Sigma rules with pySigma.
